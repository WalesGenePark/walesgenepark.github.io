Wales Gene Park is pleased to announce an exciting new partnership with Cardiff University. This partnership will focus on expanding genomic research in Wales and developing the next generation of genomic scientists.

## Partnership Objectives

- Expand genomic research in Wales
- Develop new training programs
- Create opportunities for students and early-career scientists
- Improve access to genomic technologies

This partnership marks an important milestone in our commitment to advancing genomic medicine in Wales.
