Join our team of genomics experts as they visit schools across Wales, bringing exciting hands-on activities and demonstrations to inspire students about careers in genomics and genetic research.

## Programme Overview

### Morning Sessions (09:00-12:00)

#### DNA Discovery (09:00-10:30)
- DNA Extraction Workshop
  * Hands-on practical
  * Scientific method
  * Laboratory skills
  * Results discussion

- Genetic Traits Activity
  * Population genetics
  * Inheritance patterns
  * Data collection
  * Statistical analysis

#### Career Insights (10:45-12:00)
- Meet the Scientists
  * Career paths
  * Day-to-day work
  * Educational routes
  * Skills needed

### Afternoon Sessions (13:00-15:30)

#### Technology in Action (13:00-14:15)
- Laboratory Equipment
  * PCR demonstration
  * Microscopy
  * Gel electrophoresis
  * Modern techniques

#### Future of Genomics (14:30-15:30)
- Interactive Discussion
  * Current research
  * Future possibilities
  * Ethical considerations
  * Career opportunities
