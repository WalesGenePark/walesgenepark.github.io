Celebrate International DNA Day with Wales Gene Park at our annual public engagement event. This family-friendly celebration brings genetics to life through interactive exhibits, hands-on activities, and fascinating presentations.

## Event Highlights

### Morning Session (10:00-12:30)
- DNA Extraction Workshop
  * Extract DNA from fruit
  * Learn about genetic material
  * Take home your DNA sample
- Interactive Exhibits
  * 3D chromosome models
  * Genetic traits exploration
  * DNA structure building
- Children's Activities
  * Genetic art corner
  * DNA puzzle games
  * Science storytelling

### Afternoon Session (13:30-16:00)
- Public Talks
  * "Your Genes, Your Health" - Dr. Sarah Evans
  * "The Future of Genetic Medicine" - Prof. David Hughes
  * "Genetics in Everyday Life" - Dr. Mary Williams
- Science Shows
  * "Amazing DNA" demonstration
  * Genetic inheritance games
  * Laboratory equipment showcase
- Career Corner
  * Meet genetic counselors
  * Talk to research scientists
  * Learn about genetics careers
