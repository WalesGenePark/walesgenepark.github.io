# Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2024

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better website experience and allow us to improve our site.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:
- Remember your preferences
- Understand how you use our website
- Improve our website functionality
- Provide a secure browsing experience

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
- Required for the website to function
- Cannot be disabled

### Analytics Cookies
- Help us understand how visitors use our website
- Can be disabled in your browser settings

### Functional Cookies
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Enhance your website experience

## Managing Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed.
